🐾 Zero Personal Data Collection Policy

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 25, 2026 • Last Updated: October 2026

1. Overview & Commitment to Privacy

Welcome to NyaWallet ("we", "our", or "the App"), developed by HooQii. NyaWallet was built from the ground up as a cozy, stress-free, and privacy-first financial management application.

Our fundamental architecture is 100% Offline-First. We believe your private financial records, wallet balances, debt tracking, and savings goals belong exclusively to you. We do not operate external database servers to store, analyze, or monetize your personal financial life.

🐾 The "Zero Data Collected" Guarantee

  • No User Account / No Email Collection: You do not need to sign up, log in, or provide an email address, phone number, or password to use NyaWallet.
  • No Bank Account Credentials: NyaWallet does not connect to open-banking APIs or request bank credentials. All transactions are logged locally by you.
  • No Location Tracking: We never request or collect GPS or device geolocation data.

2. Data Storage & On-Device Security

All financial entries, wallets, categories, budgets, debt records, and target goals are saved securely directly in your device's local storage (via encrypted device sandbox / local database).

If you uninstall the app or clear app data without performing a local backup, your on-device financial records will be permanently erased. You may use our built-in Local Backup & Export feature to export your data as an encrypted file or JSON format to your own personal storage.

3. Third-Party Services & Advertising (Google AdMob)

To keep NyaWallet free for everyone, the free tier of the app displays non-intrusive advertisements served through Google AdMob.

Google AdMob and its advertising partners may use device-level identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID or Apple Identifier for Advertisers IDFA) to serve ads and prevent fraudulent traffic. We integrate the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to comply with international regulations including GDPR (European Union) and CCPA/CPRA (United States):

  • You have full control over your advertising consent choices via the GDPR consent dialog in App Settings.
  • Subscribers of NyaWallet Premium enjoy a 100% ad-free experience, where ad requests are completely disabled.

4. iOS Widgets & App Groups

NyaWallet supports iOS 17 and iOS 18 Home Screen widgets. To display your daily summary and total balance on your home screen, the app shares summarized numerical values locally on your device using Apple's standard App Group container (`group.com.example.nyawallet`). No widget data is transmitted over the internet.

5. Children's Privacy

NyaWallet is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13.

6. Changes to this Policy

We may occasionally update this Privacy Policy to reflect app enhancements or legal requirements. Any modifications will be posted here with an updated effective date.
